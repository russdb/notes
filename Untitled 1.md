Although you have a PACER account, your search privileges are not activated.  
  
Your authentication token will be delivered by U.S. mail in 7-10 business days. Activation instructions are provided in the letter you receive.  
  
If you want to receive prompt access to PACER search privileges, log in to **Manage My Account** at [https://pacer.psc.uscourts.gov/pscof/login.xhtml](https://pacer.psc.uscourts.gov/pscof/login.xhtml) . Select **Activate Case Search Privileges**. Click the link in the instructions and provide your credit card information. If the card is validated, your privileges will be activated.

You may continue to log in to file and perform other activities (e.g., pay filing, admission, renewal, or other court fees), but will not be able to search.

If you would like to reactivate your search privileges, contact the PACER Service Center at (800) 676-6856 between the hours of 7 AM and 6 PM CT Monday through Friday or by email at [pacer@psc.uscourts.gov](mailto:pacer@psc.uscourts.gov).
